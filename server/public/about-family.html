One community, many roles

About the C&E Family

The C&E Family is a shared community made up of the Branch, Association, Foundation, Museum, and the people connected to them through service, support, history, and remembrance.

Stories, service, and shared history connect the C&E community across generations.

How the family fits together

The C&E Family brings together people and organizations connected through communications, electronics, signals, cyber, and the wider support network that sustains those communities. Each part has a different purpose, but all share a common aim: to connect members, preserve history, recognize service, and support one another.

Shared purpose

Whether someone arrives through military service, volunteer work, family connection, remembrance, or research, the C&E Family provides a common doorway into the community.

The four pillars

  • The Branch represents the serving military identity and professional lineage of the C&E community.
  • The Association connects serving members, veterans, families, and supporters across careers and generations.
  • The Foundation provides charitable and financial support for approved programs, heritage initiatives, and community priorities.
  • The Museum preserves and shares the artifacts, stories, and technical history that explain where the community has been.

What this site is here to do

CMCEN serves as a central gathering place for practical information from across the C&E Family. It brings together events, retirement messages, member services, historical material, community updates, and links to the organizations that make up the wider network.

The site is designed to help visitors understand how the different parts of the C&E Family relate to one another and find the information, service, or organization they need.

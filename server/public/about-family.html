C&E Family Senate

About the C&E Family

The C&E Family brings together the C&E Branch, C&E Association, Military C&E Museum Foundation, Museum, and affiliated organizations.

The C&E Senate provides a forum for the Family to work together.

A connected community

The C&E Family includes the Branch, Association, Museum and Museum Foundation, alongside affiliated organizations that work together to support C&E personnel, their families, heritage, and professional identity.

The C&E Senate Charter

The Charter sets out the Senate's role in guiding undertakings across the C&E Family. It is led by the C&E Branch Leader and Colonel Commandant, with representation from across the Family.

The C&E Family

  • The Branch represents the serving military identity and professional lineage of the C&E community.
  • The Association connects serving members, veterans, families, and supporters across careers and generations.
  • The Military C&E Museum Foundation supports the Museum and preserves the heritage of Military C&E.
  • The Museum preserves and shares the artifacts, stories, and technical history of Military C&E.

About the C&E Association

The C&E Association is the professional association for serving and retired members of the Canadian Armed Forces C&E Family.

Our membership

Former and serving Regular Force and Reserve members of the C&E Branch and its founding elements are members of the C&E Association in perpetuity. Family members may apply for Associate Membership.

Our mandate and purpose

  • Support professional development and the effectiveness of Canada's military C&E capabilities.
  • Promote the well-being of serving and retired C&E personnel and their families.
  • Preserve, educate, and celebrate C&E heritage and traditions with the C&E Museum.
  • Connect the extended C&E military community and represent it through the Conference of Defence Associations.

What we provide

The Association connects members through events, activities, and CMCEN; supports professional development; advocates for the C&E community; connects members with external morale and welfare services; and supports veterans, their families, heritage, and traditions.

Membership in the C&E Association and registration with CMCEN are free.

About the C&E Branch

The Communications and Electronics Branch provides its members with professional identity, development, camaraderie, and esprit de corps.

Background

The Communications and Electronics (C&E) Branch was created in 1968 following the integration of the Canadian Forces. It brings related occupations together in cohesive professional groups and has continued to evolve to meet the occupational and social needs of its members and related professions.

Branch support

The Branch is a professional body rather than an operational entity. A Branch Advisor is the focal point for Branch personnel and provides advice on personnel and professional matters affecting members.

The Branch Executive, led by the Branch Advisor, draws senior personnel from the Branch's varied employment environments. The Branch Chief represents the interests of non-commissioned members, while the Branch Leader represents Branch interests at higher levels of command.

Affiliated organizations

Organizations such as the Signallers Club, Lineman's Association, Air Force Telecommunications Association, and Armed Forces Communications and Electronics Association provide further identity and camaraderie. Together, they contribute to the C&E Branch mission as influential representative bodies.

About the C&E Museum & Foundation

The Military C&E Museum Foundation supports the Museum and the preservation of Military C&E heritage.

One shared mission

The Military C&E Museum Foundation is a Canada Revenue Agency-registered public foundation (Business No. 898772751RR0001). It provides charitable support for the Museum, its education program, and planned expansion and improvement projects.

Preserving C&E heritage

The Foundation preserves and perpetuates the heritage of the Military Communications and Electronics components of the Canadian Armed Forces. It supports museum facilities, projects, and programs that commemorate the Military C&E legacy and educate the public about it.

Supporting the C&E community

The Foundation receives and maintains funds to support qualified C&E Branch and affiliated-organization donees, in accordance with the Income Tax Act. Through this work, the Museum and Foundation help ensure that the stories, artifacts, and technical history of Military C&E remain available to future generations.

Board of Directors

President

John Leech

janleech@sympatico.ca

Retired in Ottawa from the CAF in June 1997 as a Maj Gen after a 33-year career in Army operations, personnel policy and communications-electronics. Postings to Europe and Egypt added variety to the mostly Ottawa, Montreal, Quebec circuit.

Career milestones include: – Royal Roads and RMC, graduating in 1964, commissioned in The Royal Canadian Corps of Signals; – Graduated from Canadian Army Staff College and National Defence College; – Commanded QGET 5 GBC and CFCC; – Service with US, UK and NATO forces; awarded CMM in 1993 Worked as a consultant for AFCEA and remains involved in volunteer work for environmental protection and at St. Helen’s Anglican Church in Ottawa.

Vice President

Greg Loos

Greg Loos retired as a MGen from the CAF and RCAF in 2018 after 33 years of service as a CELE and then General officer. Career highlights included command at the operational unit, formation, northern region, and cyber forces levels. His military career was followed by 8 years as a senior executive partner with Gartner Group, working as a strategic advisor across many Canadian enterprise sectors.

Secretary

WO Christian Marcotte, CD

WO Marcotte has served the CAF since 2001 as both a SIGINT Analyst and an ATIS Tech. He is currently posted to 76 Communication Regiment in Ottawa where he resides with his spouse.

Treasurer

Brian McDonnell

brian.mcdonnell@cmcen.ca

Brian McDonnell served the CAF after 38 years of service as a Reserve RadOp, TelOp, Regular Force Rad Tech 221, and Signals Officer.

During his career, he participated on the Op GAMESCAN 76, UNEF II, UNDOF, Ops RECUPERATION, and ABACUS. He served as Commanding Officer, 763 (Ottawa) Regiment and Commander, 70 Communication Group. He was awarded the C&E Branch Colonel-in-Chief Commendation in 2013 and the King Charles III Coronation Medal in 2025. Brian retired from Industry Canada in 2008 after many years as a Project Manager.

Director, Business Planning

Charlie Cox

ccox000@rogers.com

Charlie Cox served 22 years in the CF from 1969-1991, as a Tel Tech 223 and then in the CELE (Air) Classification. He also spent three years previously as a member of 3RHC Black Watch (Militia).

Charlie completed the UTPM program with a BEng (Electrical) from Acadia University / Technical University of Nova Scotia, and later a Master of Applied Science (Electrical Engineering) from the University of Ottawa.   His postings included HQ AFCENT (Brunssum) and CFBs Cold Lake, Penhold and Shearwater.  After retiring from the CF, with the rank of Major, Charlie worked for Ottawa area high-tech sector companies including Lockheed Martin Canada and NORTEL.  His last 10 years of employment were back in DND as the Project Engineer on Night Vision and Counter-IED projects.  Charlie retired fully in 2014, and remained in Ottawa.

Director

Bob Taylor

bat.taylor@gmail.com

Bob Taylor retired in 2009 after 33 years of full-time service in the C&E Branch as both a technician and Signal Officer.

Bob joined the CAF as a Rad Tech in 1976 and served in several units of 1CMBG as well as CFE, CFSCE and 1CDHSR before being commissioned in 1991 under the CFR Programme. He continued to serve in postings to 1 Line Tp, NDHQ J6 Ops, 1CDHSR/CFJSR, CFJOG and also returned to CFSCE and CFE. Over his career, Bob accrued 10 years of overseas postings plus operational tours in Cyprus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Afghanistan, Haiti, and Iraq. Bob was appointed an officer in the Order of Military Merit in 2005 and retired as LCol. Bob continues to serve the CAF as a contracted instructor and IMO at the Canadian Army Command and Staff College in Kingston. He plans to wrap this up in the spring of 2026, which will mark 50 consecutive years of full-time employment in support of the CAF and its people.

Director

Dave Whittier

whittier.dw@gmail.com

Retired in 2019 after 38 years service to the CAF and the Branch.

Dave joined the Communication Reserve in 1981 and transferred to the Regular Force in 1986.  He served 20 years as CELE (Land) and Signal Officer and transferred to the Army Reserve in 2006. During his time in uniform he served in eight different units, two formations, the Canadian Army Command and Staff College and the obligatory stint in Ottawa.  He had the honour of commanding in both Reg and Res environments: 742 Signal Squadron in Edmonton and 39 Signal Regiment in Vancouver.  Dave served overseas in Cyprus, former Yugoslavia, and Afghanistan.

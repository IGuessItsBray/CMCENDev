C&E Branch

Communications & Electronics Professions

The C&E Branch includes two officer professions and seven non-commissioned member professions across the Army, Air Force, and Navy.

Officer professions

  • Signals Officer
  • Communications Electronics Engineering Officer

Non-commissioned member professions

  • Signal Operator
  • Signal Technician
  • Line Technician
  • Information Systems Technician
  • Aerospace Telecommunications and Information Systems Technician
  • Signals Intelligence Specialist
  • Cyber Operator

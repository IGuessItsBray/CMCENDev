Canadian Forces Morale and Welfare Services

Programs, services, and resources that support Canadian Armed Forces members, veterans, and their families.

Serving those who serve

Canadian Forces Morale and Welfare Services (CFMWS) delivers a wide range of programs for the CAF community, including health and wellness, fitness and recreation, family support, education, financial services, and retail services.

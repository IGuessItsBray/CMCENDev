Support an exhibit

The Adopt an Exhibit campaign is offered by The Military Communications and Electronics Museum Foundation. Visit the campaign for adoption details and to make your contribution through Zeffy.

Adoption supports exhibit maintenance and cleaning for one year. The program includes a wooden plaque and online recognition; confirm recognition arrangements with the Foundation.

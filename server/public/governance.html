How the Communications and Electronics Association is constituted and governed.

Association governance

The C&E Association was incorporated as a federal not-for-profit corporation in 1970 and later continued under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act.

It is governed by a volunteer Board of Directors. The documents below define the Association’s legal structure, operating rules, and strategic direction without publishing personal rosters.

Governance

The governance framework for the Communications and Electronics Association.

Association governance

The C&E Association was incorporated as a not-for-profit corporation in 1970 and transitioned to the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act framework. It is managed by a Board of Directors with advice from subject-matter vice-presidents and ex-officio advisors.
